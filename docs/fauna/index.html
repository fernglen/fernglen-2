Fernglen Native Fauna

Fernglen is home to native birds, insects, reptiles, glow worms and fresh water fish.

Birds commonly found at Fernglen

  • Tūī
  • Piwakawaka fantail
  • Kererū wood pigeon
  • Riroriro grey warbler
  • Ruru morepork
  • Pipiwharauroa or shining cuckoo in spring and summer
  • Tauhou silvereye
  • Kotare kingfisher
  • Isolated sightings of Kākā

Other native fauna

  • Wētā
  • Skinks
  • Gecko
  • Glow worms
