# Events

The Fernglen education room is popular to hire for children’s birthday parties, meetings, yoga and more. The room is available for hire. For more please email: fernglen.nz@gmail.com

## Verran School memorial plaque for Muriel Fisher

Verran Primary School has been involved with the garden for many years and planted a kōwhai tree in the rock garden area in Muriel's honour when she died in 2012. On 23rd May a group of students and teachers mounted a memorial plaque near the kōwhai in memory of Muriel Fisher.

You can read an article about {{< link text="the event on stuff.co.nz" href="http://www.stuff.co.nz/dominion-post/news/national/92902022/Botanist-from-Aucklands-North-Shore-passes-on-love-of-natives-even-from-grave" >}}.

It's great to see the younger generation showing interest in native plants and the continuous support of Fernglen Gardens by Verran School. Very much appreciated!

## Working Bees

Working bees are currently taking place on the **2nd Saturday** of each month. They usually run from **9am to 12noon** and involve maintenance of the gardens as well as planting. We meet inside the gate area/in front of the education room. If you are interested in keeping the Fernglen Gardens in good shape and while doing that learn more about native plants, you are very welcome to join in. We are currently only a small group of people and any additional help is very much appreciated.

Volunteers can assist during the week also.

## Open Days

Fernglen has an Open day once a year in summer, during Ecofest. Members of the public are invited to come along and enjoy:

- A guided botanical tour
- Sale of ‘not so easy’ to find native plants
- Information on ‘how and where’ to plant native plants
- Botanical journaling, illustrating with works for sale 
- Conservation themed activities for children i.e. bird trail, art activities

## Art Classes

Botanical Art classes are available one Sunday a month.
